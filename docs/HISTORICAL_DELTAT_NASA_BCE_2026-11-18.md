# Bounded historical ΔT estimates — 2026-11-18

**Experimental opt-in.** No default model, UTC/UT1 conversion or civil-time policy is selected. Both APIs accept an explicit astronomical decimal UT1 year and return seconds. Neither returns an angular error bound.

`espenak_meeus_2006_extended` evaluates the uncorrected [NASA-published Espenak–Meeus polynomial](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/LEcat5/deltatpoly.html) for `-1999.0 <= year < 3001.0`: astronomical year −1999 is 2000 BCE and year 0 is 1 BCE. For years before −500 it uses the publication's long-term parabola; from −500 to 500 it uses its published sixth-degree fit. For nonnegative years it calls the unchanged `espenak_meeus_2006_long_range`; that older API still rejects BCE inputs. The eclipse-canon lunar secular-acceleration correction is not applied, and post-2005 values are old projections, not current observations. Years before −1999 or after 3000 require a different explicit source; no extrapolation is silently performed.

`morrison_stephenson_2004_standard_error` returns the [NASA-described Morrison–Stephenson estimated standard error](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEhelp/uncertainty2004.html), conservatively bounded to decimal years `[-999, 1200]` corresponding to the source's 1000 BCE–1200 CE period. This is a statistical model estimate, **not a guaranteed interval**. It is independently opt-in: callers must preserve provenance and decide whether combining it with a particular ΔT estimate is meaningful. Outside that range it errors, even if a ΔT estimate is available. The source's year notation has an endpoint ambiguity between its text and table; the conservative bound does not infer an additional BCE year.

NASA's dates cover far less than the product goal of 13000 BCE–3999 CE. Neither estimate establishes physical truth, total time-scale error, TT–TDB approximation error or position uncertainty; ancient observations and historical local-clock records have their own uncertainties. The newer Morrison et al. 2021 S5 candidate and USNO observations were studied on a separate branch: source coefficients are not bundled, one 1514 conditional constraint did not pass, and no verified automatic splice is available. They are not silently activated here.

Unit tests check the published polynomial branches, historical standard-error anchors, endpoints, nonfinite input, and bit-for-bit parity with the existing nonnegative-year API. These are source-model consistency and compatibility checks, not independent historical measurements or DE441 validation.
