# DE441 package orientation extension — plan before new comparisons

Do not change the ordinary orientation guard or activate an app engine. Evaluate the already existing explicitly experimental long-term path; it uses Vondrák pole-based mean ecliptic/equator, IAU 2006 frame bias and the 77-term series with full IERS polynomial arguments, which is **not** standard IAU 2000B. Its TT/TDB inputs, rate stencil and physical accuracy remain experimental. The ordinary model's numerical guard and 0.01-day derivative margin are separate from package coverage.

## Independent model implementation

At four dates (January, April, July, October, 12:00 TT) every 25 years from astronomical year 0 through 2300, export the existing Rust long-term pole matrices and experimental nutation. Compare the mean-equator matrix and ecliptic pole with ERFA `ltp` / `ltpecl` using the same Vondrák model: diagnostic maximum element/pole tolerance 1e-12. Compare the extrapolated 77-term nutation with ERFA IAU 2000A `nut00a`; separately compare the linear-argument ERFA 2000B `nut00b` with 2000A. Predeclared investigative component limits are 0.05″ for the experimental series and 5″ for unguarded standard 2000B, not product or physical-accuracy requirements. Report per-era maxima and all exceedances; 2000A, 2000B and the experimental series are correlated theoretical models and all extrapolate.

## Actual packages and JPL

Read the local manifest v1 and verify the declared SHA-256 and byte size of all ten existing slice files and the full source before and after; do not modify, move or distribute them. Use the 20 samples at each package's logical start+1 and end-exclusive−1 TDB day, plus the standard orientation guard's four inside/outside probe dates and J2000 and JD TDB 2461362.0. For each of the ten supported targets evaluate the existing experimental pole-based apparent ecliptic six fields from the source; evaluate all 200 package-specific samples from the selected slice and compare its six fields with the source. Before running fix per-component package identity limits: 1e-8 degrees or degrees/TDB-day and 1e-12 AU or AU/TDB-day. This is not a JPL or physical validation.

At these 26 epochs and ten targets, obtain Horizons Q45 airless geocentric ICRF and Q20 distance, with TT epochs from the existing Rust conversion, and rotate each Q45 through ERFA Vondrák pole ecliptic, IAU 2006 J2000 bias and full 2000A longitude nutation. This independent implementation of a *different* nutation model yields a cross-model diagnostic; fixed beforehand: 1 arcsecond per longitude/latitude component, 0.1 arcsecond/TT day for each angular rate, 5e-10 AU/TT day for radial rate and 1e-9 AU for distance. Derive rates from printed JPL positions at ±0.02, ±0.04 and ±0.08 day using fourth-order stencils with **actual requested TT** abscissae. These are exploratory limits, not assertions that either Earth-orientation model is physically correct. Preserve exceptions and failures; mark near-solar cases as numerical stress tests. Q20 direct radial speed remains a separate previously failed convention comparison, not a replacement for the range derivative.

At the four guard-neighbor dates, compare the existing standard and experimental results on the two accepted standard-side samples and verify fail-closed on the two rejected samples. Record the potential switch discontinuity without inserting an automatic switch. Also compare candidate long-term apparent longitudes to Horizons Q31 only as a distinct IAU76/80/Owen convention comparison, not an equal-frame accuracy gate. Store reference URLs and raw responses only in new owner-local reports and do not claim physical Earth-orientation certainty or redistribute data.

After the first same-epoch whole-pipeline comparison, isolate the frame contribution as a **post-hoc descriptive diagnostic**: construct ERFA Vondrák and IAU 2006 ecliptic matrices using the same full IAU 2000A nutation at each of the six control dates, then calculate their relative rotation. Set no new pass threshold. The existing long-term Rust path also refines the light-time emission epoch, so its difference from the standard path is not solely a frame difference; neither diagnostic establishes physical model accuracy or an acceptable transition.

As a separately planned local check after the first ±1-day package samples, evaluate the experimental path on each package at its inclusive logical start, one second after its start, and one second before its exclusive end, for all ten targets: 300 slice/source comparisons. Use the same 1e-8 degree/degree-per-day and 1e-12 AU/AU-per-day component limits, verify every input hash before/after, and record all failures. These are direct readers of individually supplied slice files, not a test of mobile package selection or app activation.
